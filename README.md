## 🎯 Project Overview

This efficient mobile food ordering application aims at reducing waiting time and ordering time in school and university cafeterias. By digitizing the ordering process, students can place orders quickly and conveniently, allowing them to focus on their studies.

## 🎓 Objectives

- **Reduce cafeteria line wait times** - Minimize time spent in lines before and after ordering food
- **Improve service delivery** - Enhance overall cafeteria operations and customer experience
- **Maximize study time** - Especially during exam periods when quick meals are essential

## ✨ Features

- 🛒 **Shopping Cart** - Add and manage food items easily
- ⭐ **Meal Ratings** - Rate meals and provide feedback
- 📦 **Quantity Management** - Adjust item quantities before checkout
- 🔍 **Advanced Search** - Filter and search meals by category, price, or name
- 🍴 **Browse Menu** - View all available meals with descriptions
- 💳 **Secure Ordering** - Place orders securely through the app

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Platform**: Android Mobile
- **Languages**: 
  - Java
  - Kotlin
- **IDE**: Android Studio
